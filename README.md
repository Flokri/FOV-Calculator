# Modern FOV Calculator (enhanced)

Field of view and triple screen angle calculator for sim racing.

- **Find my FOV**: enter your screens and seating distance, get the FOV for each game and the angle for your side screens.
- **Find my distance**: enter your screens and the angle or FOV you want (including a game's own setting), get the seating distance that makes it correct.
- **Visualization**: top view of the rig with the field of view and side screen angle, plus a side view with the vertical FOV.
- Single, triple, flat and curved screens, bezels, cm or inches, and a shareable link that keeps your setup.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step. The math lives in `calc.js` and can be loaded in Node for testing.
